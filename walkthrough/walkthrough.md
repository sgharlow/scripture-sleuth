## Complete Gameplay Flow

### 1. New User Experience
- User opens a Scripture Sleuth puzzle post on r/ScriptureSleuth
- Sees the Welcome Screen with parchment-styled theme
- Instructions explain: Read 5 verses, spot the fake, learn real scripture
- Click "Begin Your Quest" to start

### 2. Playing the Puzzle
- Presented with 5 Bible verse cards
- Each card shows verse reference, text, and theme
- Tap any verse to mark it as your suspect
- Theme: Wisdom, Love, Faith, Hope, etc.

### 3. Making Your Selection
- Tapping a verse marks it visually (highlighted border)
- "Lock In Your Answer" button appears
- Can change selection before locking in

### 4. Confirmation
- "Final Decision" modal appears
- Shows the verse you selected
- "This cannot be undone" warning
- Choose "Go Back" or "Confirm"

### 5. Results
- If correct: "Found It!" celebration
- See the fake verse explanation (what made it fake)
- View real verse connections to subreddits
- See community stats and your streak
- Achievement unlocks shown as toasts

### 6. Completed State
- Return visits show "Today's Puzzle Complete"
- Displays your choice and streak
- Leaderboard rankings (Streak and Accuracy)
- Countdown to next puzzle (midnight UTC)

## Key UI Elements

### Parchment Theme
- Background: #faf3e3 (warm parchment)
- Primary: #722f37 (burgundy/wine)
- Accent: #c9a227 (gold)
- Font: Georgia serif for verses

### Verse Cards
- Book icon with verse reference
- Full verse text in serif font
- Theme tag (Wisdom, Love, etc.)
- Selected state: gold border highlight

### Result Screen
- Fake verse explanation with reasoning
- Subreddit connections for real verses
- Community vote distribution
- Achievement notifications

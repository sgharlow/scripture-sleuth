# Scripture Sleuth - Submission Video Script

**Target Length:** 1-2 minutes (180-240 words when read aloud)
**Style:** Engaging, clear, demonstrates daily game loop

---

## VIDEO SCRIPT

### Opening (10 sec)
**[Show game logo / r/ScriptureSleuth subreddit]**

"Welcome to Scripture Sleuth — the daily Bible verse mystery game where your task is simple: find the fake among the faithful."

---

### Concept (20 sec)
**[Show Welcome screen with "ONE VERSE ISN'T REAL" tagline]**

"Every day, you're presented with five Bible verses on a common theme. Four are real scripture. One is a convincing fake that sounds biblical but doesn't actually exist. Your job? Spot the imposter. You get one guess — choose wisely."

---

### Unique Feature: Subreddit Connections (20 sec)
**[Show result reveal with subreddit connections]**

"Here's the twist: each real verse connects to a real subreddit. 'Love your neighbor' links to r/HumansBeingBros. 'Judge not' connects to r/AmItheAsshole. After you guess, you discover these surprising connections — it's education meets entertainment, Reddit-style."

---

### Daily Loop (15 sec)
**[Show difficulty badge, streak counter, timer]**

"New puzzles drop at midnight UTC. Difficulty scales through the week — Monday is easy, weekends are expert level. Build your streak, climb the leaderboard, and unlock eight achievements from 'First Discovery' to '30-Day Streak.'"

---

### Community Features (15 sec)
**[Show contribution form, leaderboard]**

"Players can submit their own fake verse ideas for future puzzles. Vote on community contributions. Compete on accuracy and streak leaderboards."

---

### Closing (10 sec)
**[Show game in action, final logo]**

"Scripture Sleuth — 126 puzzles ready, four months of daily content. One verse isn't real. Can you find it? Play now at r/ScriptureSleuth."

## RECORDING TIPS

1. **Pace:** Slightly slower than conversational, ~140 words/minute
2. **Emphasis:** Stress "ONE guess" and "subreddit connections"
3. **Energy:** Enthusiastic but not over-the-top
4. **B-roll:** Screen recordings can fill gaps in narration
5. **Music:** Light, curious background music (royalty-free)
